Before you join

Questions, answered.

Do I need coding experience to join a workshop?

No. Workshops are built for complete beginners. If you can use a web browser and have an idea, you can build with AI.

What do I need to bring to a workshop?

A laptop, a stable internet connection, a free Google account, and an idea you want to build. You must be 18 or older.

Are workshops recorded?

No. Workshops are live-only, with real-time questions and help. When registration opens, read the specific Eventbrite listing for its refund terms before purchasing.

How do I know if consulting is right for my business?

Request a free business discovery call to discuss a project. For personal teaching or task guidance, use the separate personal AI help inquiry. A free call does not include a paid lesson.

What size clients do you work with?

Mostly small businesses, solopreneurs, and community organizations. Larger engagements happen through my program management work with enterprise clients — reach out if that's what you need.

How do payments and invoicing work?

Workshop registration and payment will use Eventbrite when the confirmed event links are available. Read the event listing for total price, fees, refund terms, and joining instructions. Business consulting is invoiced after scope is agreed. Personal-session pricing and payment arrangements are confirmed before booking.

Will I need paid AI tools?

Bring a laptop, a stable internet connection, a free Google account, and an idea. The confirmed event listing will identify the tools, accounts, and any extra software costs required. Check those details before buying a ticket.

Will my app be published by the end?

We work on a simple web app. App Store publishing is not covered, and publishing online depends on the project, tools, and your setup.
