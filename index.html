07 · Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

Do I need coding experience to join a workshop?

No. Workshops are built for complete beginners. If you can use a web browser and have an idea, you can build with AI.

What do I need to bring to a workshop?

A laptop, a stable internet connection, a free Google account, and an idea you want to build. You must be 18 or older.

Are workshops recorded?

No — workshops are live-only. That way you get real-time help and real-time feedback. Refund policy is listed on each Eventbrite listing.

How do I know if consulting is right for my business?

Book a free 30-minute Discovery Call. We'll talk through your business, where you're stuck, and whether what I offer is a fit. No obligation.

What size clients do you work with?

Mostly small businesses, solopreneurs, and community organizations. Larger engagements happen through my program management work with enterprise clients — reach out if that's what you need.

How do payments and invoicing work?

Workshops are paid at registration via Eventbrite. Consulting engagements are invoiced after the Discovery Call once we agree on scope. All work is covered by a simple written agreement.

Get notified about the next workshop.

No spam. Just a short note when a new cohort opens.
