No. Workshops are built for complete beginners. If you can use a web browser and have an idea, you can build with AI.

What do I need to bring to a workshop?

A laptop, a stable internet connection, a free Google account, and an idea you want to build. You must be 18 or older.

No. Workshops are live-only, with real-time questions and help. Read the Eventbrite listing for refund terms before buying.

Reserve the free demo through Luma. Register and pay for the December class through Eventbrite. Read the event listing for the total price, fees, refund terms, and joining instructions.

Will I need paid AI tools?

No. We use free tools only. No paid subscription required.

Will my website be published by the end?

We’ll build, check, and publish a simple business website together. The paid class includes follow-up help for publishing problems.
