## 3. Positioning recommendation [OPINION]
**Positioning line:** *"I help beginners and small-business owners turn an idea into a working app with AI, in plain English and without writing code."*
- Audience: complete beginners, solopreneurs and small-business owners in Miami and online.
- Proof angle (true facts only): 10+ years leading tech projects; 2 apps in the Apple App Store; built an AI website builder for small businesses; AI Practitioner Certificate, Miami Dade College.
- Brand: keep the domain for now, but put your name and face first ("Joane Aristilde" / Tali, whichever you go by publicly). People book a person, not "Ahmir & Jael Co." The LLC name can sit in the footer.
- Drop for now: Finance ops, CRM, data-center pivot, the TPM label. They confuse this audience. (If you're job hunting, keep those on LinkedIn instead.)

## 6. Connecting Eventbrite and booking
- **Eventbrite** [FACT: tickets sold there]: Link every "Save my seat" button straight to the Nov 21 event URL, and every demo button to the Oct 29 event URL. Add `?aff=website` to the end of the links so Eventbrite reports show which sales came from the site. Optional: Eventbrite's embedded checkout widget (it requires pasting their script; test it on mobile). In the Eventbrite listing, put your site link in the organizer profile.
- **Calendly** [FACT, calendly.com/pricing, 2026-10-14]: The free plan includes one event type, one calendar connection, a booking page, and one-on-one scheduling. Payments (Stripe/PayPal) and reminders need Standard ($10/seat/mo, billed yearly), so that costs money and needs your approval. Recommendation: one free event, "Free 20-min AI chat". Paste the link on the "Book a free call" buttons. Take payment for paid sessions later, by invoice.
- **Email signup** [OPINION]: Netlify Forms works on Netlify sites with no extra service (add `data-netlify="true"` to a form). Check the current free limits before relying on it.
- **Socials/LinkedIn:** Link the site in your LinkedIn Featured section and in the "The TPM Who Builds" newsletter footer.
